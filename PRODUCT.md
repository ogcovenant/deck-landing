# Deck Cards

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User specified Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Motion and Lucide. Build in this repository. The existing brand presentation is reference material, preserved in `../deck brand/`.

## Users

Individuals managing personal cards and organizations assigning payment cards to employees. Cardholders can access personal and assigned work cards in the same mobile app.

## Product Purpose

A responsive early-access marketing site for Deck Cards. Explain personal and business cards, the unified app and spending controls; invite early-access interest.

## Positioning

Every card. One Deck. Personal and assigned business cards share an app while ownership, balances and permissions remain separate.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The user confirms Deck Cards is available and requires present-tense product copy, while explicitly retaining “Get early access” as the CTA. Describe NGN and USD virtual cards, freeze/unfreeze, transaction visibility and spending controls directly. Organizations issue, assign, control, freeze and terminate their cards. Do not add coming-soon language, planned-feature footnotes or visible prototype/illustrative labels. No verified fees, issuance timings, networks, partners or testimonials. No future financial products belong on this page.

## Brand Commitments

Use the supplied Riff identity, original SVG logos, Hanken Grotesk display font and Noto Sans financial/body font. Follow the brand folder's colors and geometry. User explicitly delegates routine design decisions and requests exceptional card designs and realistic product UI. For this landing page, the user specifically requests premium layout and typographic hierarchy; generous editorial scale and spacing guide the current surface.

## Evidence on Hand

The user brief and `../deck brand/brand/`, `../deck brand/DESIGN.md`, local fonts and SVG artwork. Product demonstrations use authored example names, amounts and organizations; the user explicitly requested removing visible prototype labels. No waitlist provider or approved legal policies supplied. These demos do not change real cards, and signup must still require confirmed provider acceptance.

## Product Principles

- Demonstrate unified access with separate ownership.
- Use present-tense product copy and retain the user's “Get early access” CTA.
- Never imply an email was registered without confirmed server acceptance.
- Preserve the brand and avoid fabricated financial claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic sections, keyboard navigation, visible focus, accessible forms and segmented controls, reduced motion and mobile reflow.
